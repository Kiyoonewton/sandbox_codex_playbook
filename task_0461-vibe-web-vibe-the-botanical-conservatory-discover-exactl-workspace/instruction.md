# The plant details view doesn't hold together

## What you see

When you tap a plant to open its detail panel, the care information doesn't stay where it belongs. Text and details spill out past the edge of the panel, running over whatever is behind it instead of staying neatly contained. It makes the panel look broken and the care notes hard to read, since part of the content sits outside the boundary that's supposed to frame it.

## What correct looks like

Opening a plant's details should present a tidy, self-contained panel where every bit of care information sits comfortably inside its frame, readable at a glance and never bleeding past the edges.
