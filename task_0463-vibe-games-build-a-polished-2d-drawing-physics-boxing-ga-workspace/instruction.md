# Pausing mid-punch leaves a ghost line behind

If I hit pause while my glove is still flying toward the target and then resume, the old line is still sitting there on screen and won't go away, even though nothing I do afterward seems connected to it anymore.

It's not just the line either. The crates and the other boxer stay wherever they happened to be at the moment I paused, instead of settling back into their normal spots for the level. It leaves the whole screen feeling like it's stuck half-finished from a punch that never actually landed or missed.
